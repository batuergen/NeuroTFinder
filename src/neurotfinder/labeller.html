MRI sequence and plane labelling

Thank you for helping. You will see brain images, one at a time. For each one, please choose:

  • Sequence: T1, T1 with contrast, T2, FLAIR, CT or other. If it is T1-weighted but you cannot tell from this single slice whether contrast was given, choose T1, contrast unclear.
  • Plane: axial, coronal or sagittal.

Please do not guess. If you are not sure, give your best answer and tick Not sure; that is useful information too. If an image looks abnormal, or is not a usable brain image at all, tick Looks abnormal or add a short note.

The images are in random order and carry no diagnosis. Your progress is saved after every image, so you can close this page and continue later. Every images there is a short break screen.

When you have finished, press Download results and email the file it saves.
